Auto-trade (Olymp Trade DEMO account only)
Tick "Auto-trade" on a candle alert for an Olymp Trade asset. When it fires and the switch below is ON, one demo trade is sent in the signal's direction. It can only ever send demo trades. Each trade's result is read back about a minute after it ends (an estimate from 1-minute candles; your Olymp demo history is the truth) and compared with the pretend trades in the Results card.
Enter your demo balance once, in the SAME units as the Stake (if a stake of 1500 shows as 15.00 on the screen, a balance of 10,000.00 is 1000000 here). The app keeps it up to date from the estimated results of the trades it sends, so re-enter it now and then to correct drift. The % is limited to 0.1 to 25.
Auto-trade switches itself OFF at either limit and tells your phone. Results come from Olymp's 1-minute candles (an estimate), so it reacts about a minute after the last trade ends. Switching it on again starts a fresh streak.
Adaptive direction (confirmation cases)
Each case keeps score of its default direction on its last confirmed trades. When we are 95% sure the default direction wins less than half, that case trades the other way, and it switches back when the default recovers. Case 1: same as the signal. Case 2: opposite. Case 3: same.
Playbook (learns which setups keep working)
From your finished confirmed trades it looks for setups (one candle characteristic, or two together) that win clearly more than half the time, on older AND newer trades, and in every part of the history. Only rules that pass all of that become ACTIVE. Each active rule is checked against its latest trades; when it stops working it is retired, and a new pattern that passes the same tests is learned automatically. Pretend trades keep being recorded either way, so it keeps learning.
Entry signals to my phone: "GET READY" as soon as a setup that an active rule could still match forms (with the exact entry time), a 30-second reminder, then "ENTER asset UP/DOWN now, expiry at HH:MM" if the confirmation candle qualifies. Works even with auto-trade OFF.
